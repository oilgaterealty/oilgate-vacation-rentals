House rules

Door code sent 2:45 PM · Check-in 3:00 PM · Checkout 11:00 AM (Akron local time)

  1. Safety is our number one priority: For any emergencies, please call 911. For any safety hazards that may arise, please notify management immediately.
  2. No parties in the home: Guests are limited to 3 visitors at a time total in the home. No overnight stays are allowed unless previously approved and paid for.
  3. No cigarette smoking in the home.
  4. Park in designated areas only: Unit 2 guests are permitted to use the driveway. Unit 1 guests are required to park on the street. If needed, feel free to communicate with Unit 1 guests regarding parking schedules to avoid any block-in situations.
  5. Remove laundry from washer/dryer immediately after the cycle completes.
  6. No pets are allowed: This property is pet-free.
  7. Trash and recycling: Trash and recycling day is Sunday night for pickup on Monday morning. Please ensure all trash and recycling are placed in the appropriate bins and that the bins are placed at the curb and removed at these times. Please do not park in front of the trashcans Sunday night or Monday morning.
  8. Use of appliances: Please use all appliances and amenities appropriately, and notify management if anything is malfunctioning.
  9. Check-In/Check-Out: Please adhere to the check-in and check-out times unless prior arrangements have been made. Follow all check-out instructions provided.
  10. No alterations to the property: Guests should not make any alterations to the property, such as rearranging furniture, without permission.
  11. No excessive noise: In addition to quiet hours, guests should avoid excessive noise at all times.
