Am I booking a room or a whole place?

You are booking an entire Oakdale unit or the Parkgate home. Individual bedrooms are not offered through this site. Oakdale’s basement laundry is shared between units.

How many people can stay?

Each property allows up to 6 overnight guests, and everyone staying must be listed on the booking. Up to 3 non-overnight visitors may visit at a time. Parties and events are not allowed.

What currency and deposit should I expect?

Prices are presented in USD. The security deposit is shown at checkout. Review the complete stay total and the collection and release terms at checkout.

When are check-in and check-out?

The current listings show check-in at 3 PM and check-out at 11 AM, Akron local time. Confirm your arrival arrangements with your host.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellation terms depend on the live Lodgify quote. Read the cancellation terms displayed for your dates at checkout before confirming.

Can I ask about a longer stay?

Yes. Contact your host through the Lodgify inquiry or booking flow to discuss your plans and available dates. Rates and availability depend on the property and stay dates.
