House rules

Check-in 03:00 PM · Check-out 11:00 AM (Akron local time)

  1. Safety is our number one priority: For any emergencies, please call 911. For any safety hazards that may arise, please notify management immediately.
  2. No parties in the home: Guests are limited to 3 visitors at a time total in the home. No overnight stays are allowed unless previously approved and paid for.
  3. No cigarette smoking in the home.
  4. Quiet hours are between 10pm and 10am: Please be respectful of your neighbors.
  5. Be respectful the space: Please clean up after yourself.
  6. Park in designated areas only: Feel free to use the street or driveway for parking. The garage is not in use and please do not park on the grass.
  7. No pets are allowed: This property is pet-free.
  8. Trash and recycling: Trash and recycling day is Sunday night for pickup on Monday morning. Please ensure all trash and recycling are placed in the appropriate bins and that the bins are placed at the curb and removed at these times. Please do not park in front of the trashcans Sunday night or Monday morning.
  9. Use of appliances: Please use all appliances and amenities appropriately, and notify management if anything is malfunctioning.
  10. Check-In/Check-Out: Please adhere to the check-in and check-out times unless prior arrangements have been made. Follow all check-out instructions provided.
  11. No alterations to the property: Guests should not make any alterations to the property, such as rearranging furniture, without permission.
  12. No excessive noise: In addition to quiet hours, guests should avoid excessive noise at all times.
