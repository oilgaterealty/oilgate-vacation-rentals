House rules

Check-in 03:00 PM · Check-out 11:00 AM (Akron local time)

  1. Safety is our number one priority: For any emergencies, please call 911. For any safety hazards that may arise, please notify management immediately.
  2. No parties in the home: Guests are limited to 3 visitors at a time total in the home. No overnight stays are allowed unless previously approved and paid for.
  3. No cigarette smoking in the home.
  4. Quiet hours are between 10 pm and 10 am: Please be respectful of guests in the other unit and neighbors.
  5. Laundry room: The basement laundry room is used by both units. Please clean up after yourself.
  6. Park in designated areas only: Unit 1 guests must use street parking. The driveway is reserved for Unit 2 guests. Please do not block the driveway or park in a way that prevents Unit 2 guests from entering or exiting.
  7. Remove laundry from washer/dryer immediately after the cycle completes.
  8. No pets are allowed: This property is pet-free.
  9. Trash and recycling: Trash and recycling day is Sunday night for pickup on Monday morning. Please ensure all trash and recycling are placed in the appropriate bins and that the bins are placed at the curb and removed at these times. Please do not park in front of the trashcans Sunday night or Monday morning.
  10. Use of appliances: Please use all appliances and amenities appropriately, and notify management if anything is malfunctioning.
  11. Check-In/Check-Out: Please adhere to the check-in and check-out times unless prior arrangements have been made. Follow all check-out instructions provided.
  12. No alterations to the property: Guests should not make any alterations to the property, such as rearranging furniture, without permission.
  13. No excessive noise: In addition to quiet hours, guests should avoid excessive noise at all times.
