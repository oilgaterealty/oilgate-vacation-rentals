The rental

Inside Oakdale Unit 2

Welcome to Unit 2, a spacious upper-level unit for short and mid-term stays. Three bedrooms, each with a comfortable bed, a workspace with a plush chair, and FireTV with free cable, plus closets, dressers, and blackout curtains. Your unit has its own private, fully equipped kitchen, cozy living room, and dining room. A washer and dryer are in the basement laundry room, the only space shared with the other unit. Ideal for professionals, students, or anyone seeking privacy in Akron.

Sleeping: 2 Double bed · 1 Queen bed

Before you book

Guests 18+ to book.

  • Safety is our number one priority: For any emergencies, contact emergency services. For any safety hazards that may arise, please notify management immediately.
  • No parties in the home: Guests are limited to 3 visitors at a time total in the home. No overnight stays are allowed unless previously approved and paid for.
  • No cigarette smoking in the home.
  • Quiet hours are between 10 pm and 10 am: Please be respectful of guests in the other unit and neighbors.
  • Laundry room: The basement laundry room is used by both units. Please clean up after yourself.
  • Park in designated areas only: Unit 2 guests are permitted to use the driveway. Unit 1 guests are required to park on the street. If needed, feel free to communicate with Unit 1 guests regarding parking schedules to avoid any block-in situations.
  • Remove laundry from washer/dryer immediately after the cycle completes.
  • No pets are allowed: This property is pet-free.
  • Trash and recycling: Trash and recycling day is Sunday night for pickup on Monday morning. Please ensure all trash and recycling are placed in the appropriate bins and that the bins are placed at the curb and removed at these times. Please do not park in front of the trashcans Sunday night or Monday morning.
  • Use of appliances: Please use all appliances and amenities appropriately, and notify management if anything is malfunctioning.
  • Check-In/Check-Out: Please adhere to the check-in and check-out times unless prior arrangements have been made. Follow all check-out instructions provided.
  • No alterations to the property: Guests should not make any alterations to the property, such as rearranging furniture, without permission.
  • No excessive noise: In addition to quiet hours, guests should avoid excessive noise at all times.

Guests are responsible for damage they cause. Oilgate Realty is not liable for injury or loss of personal property, except as required by law.
