House rules and terms

Check-in and check-out
Check in from 3:00 PM. Check out by 11:00 AM.
Guests 18+
The person booking must be 18 or older.
No parties
Up to 3 visitors at a time. No overnight visitors unless approved and paid for in advance.
Quiet hours
10 PM to 10 AM, out of respect for neighbors and the other unit.
Smoke-free and pet-free
No cigarette smoking in the home. No pets.
Parking
Unit 2 uses the driveway, Unit 1 parks on the street. Parkgate guests can use the street or the driveway.
Laundry
Oakdale's basement laundry is shared by both units. Please clean up and empty the machines when done.
Damage
Guests are responsible for damage they cause. Oilgate Realty is not liable for injury or loss of personal property, except as required by law.

Questions, answered

Am I booking a room or a whole place?

A whole place. You book an entire Oakdale unit or the whole Parkgate home. At Oakdale, only the basement laundry room is shared with the other unit.

Are there cleaning fees?

No cleaning fees, ever, unless house rules are broken.

How old do I need to be to book?

18 or older.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 3:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM.

Can I bring a pet?

No. All three homes are pet-free.
