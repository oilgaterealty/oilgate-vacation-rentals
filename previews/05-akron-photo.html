Good to know

Questions

Am I booking a room or a whole place?

A whole place. You book an entire Oakdale unit or the whole Parkgate home. At Oakdale, only the basement laundry room is shared with the other unit.

Are there cleaning fees?

No cleaning fees, ever, unless house rules are broken.

How old do I need to be to book?

18 or older.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 3:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM.

Can I bring a pet?

No. All three homes are pet-free.
