## What makes it recognizable
- **Layout:** thin dark promo strip on top, transparent header over a full-bleed lifestyle photo, centered serif headline with a spaced-caps subline, and an **inline white search bar** (destination, check-in, check-out, one strong colored button) right in the hero. "Homes that inspire" intro on the left with a short rule, portrait home cards on the right with a ribbon tag ("Premier") and serif names over a dark gradient. Alternating photo/text feature rows. Collections carousel, "As seen in" logo marquee, a closing CTA band, a big locations footer.
- **Nav pattern:** search bar becomes sticky in the header after scrolling.
- **Type:** old-style serif headlines (close free match: EB Garamond), clean sans body (match: Montserrat).
- **Type scale:** hero ~80px desktop / ~44px phone, section heads ~46px, body 16px.
- **Palette:** near-black navy, white, magenta button. Rebuilt with OVR navy and the gold as the button color.
- **Spacing:** airy, centered max-width ~1160px.
- **Motion:** word-by-word headline reveals, carousels, logo marquee. Rebuild keeps a gentle card zoom on hover.
- **Images:** warm lifestyle hero, tall portrait home cards.

## Notes
- Key's own headlines were not copied; the rebuild uses new OVR wording.
- No press logos in the rebuild (OVR has none to show truthfully).
