# LaVante's Akron theme (oilgatelinks.com/app) → Design 4 "Akron Linked"

- Source named in the brief: https://oilgatelinks.com/app
- What happened: the page redirects to a sign-in screen. I did not sign in, so the theme was rebuilt from the brief's description: big Akron photo background, deep navy and blue tones, clean centered white type, a glassy card, a social-icon row.
- Not used, as instructed: the portrait of his grandmother and his own photo.

## How it was rebuilt
- **Layout:** one centered column (620px) like a link-in-bio page, widening to three cards on desktop. Glass booking card at the very top, a giant "AKRON" word, the stays as big link buttons with thumbnails, a gold promise card, glass panels for compare, rules and FAQ.
- **Background:** fixed downtown Akron photo (Lock 3 and the canal at dusk) under a navy-to-deep-blue gradient.
- **Type:** Anton for the big word and section heads, Poppins for body.
- **Glass:** rgba white 12% fill, 28% white border, 14px backdrop blur, 22px radius.
- **Social row:** round glass icon buttons for Facebook, Instagram, X and email.
