# Best of → Design 6 "Oilgate Signature"

Takes the strongest pieces of designs 1-3 and dresses them in design 4's imagery.

| Piece | From |
|---|---|
| Serif headline over a full photo, inline search bar in the hero, ribbon portrait cards, intro with a gold rule | Key (3) |
| Calm stone sections, day timeline, floating Book button, serif FAQ with + / – | Schlosshotel (1) |
| Marquee strip, condensed display labels, arch-masked photo | Club Charlestown (2) |
| Navy-washed Akron photo hero and footer, glass booking card, round glass social icons | Akron Linked (4) |

- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond headlines, Inter body, Bebas Neue labels.
- **Why these:** the Key search bar is the clearest booking pattern; Schlosshotel's timeline answers check-in, quiet hours and check-out at a glance; Club Charlestown's marquee carries the cleaning promise and 18+ rule without extra reading; the Akron photo gives a local identity the home photos alone don't.
