## Design
- **Layout:** edge-to-edge city hero with giant uppercase type; a gold booking strip directly under it; a 12-column photo mosaic with captions and credits on each photo; stays as alternating photo pairs on charcoal; a skyline panorama band with the cleaning promise; heavy-rule compare, rules and FAQ.
- **Type:** Archivo 400-800 (grotesk, tight tracking for display).
- **Palette:** charcoal #101418, paper #f5f3ee, navy, gold.
