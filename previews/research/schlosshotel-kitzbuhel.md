## What makes it recognizable
- **Layout:** full-bleed photo hero with the wordmark centered near the top; small-caps tagline under it ("THE SPA MOMENTUM"). Intro block sits between two framed photos. Rooms come as a horizontal slider ("For people of refined taste. For you.") with Book / Enquire / Details on every card. Director quote on a solid color panel beside a photo. "First / Second / Third" storytelling blocks. A day timeline (5.45 a.m., 8.55 a.m., 10 a.m. …) with a photo per moment. Big footer with three photo tiles.
- **Nav pattern:** hamburger plus phone icon top corners. Book and Enquire are fixed buttons: bottom right on desktop, a full-width bottom bar on phones. A scalloped round offer badge floats over the hero bottom left.
- **Type:** high-contrast serif for headlines (close free match: Cormorant Garamond), clean geometric sans for body and spaced caps labels (match: Jost).
- **Type scale:** hero wordmark ~90px desktop, section heads ~48px, body ~17px, labels 12-13px with wide tracking.
- **Palette:** deep burgundy / aubergine, white, warm stone. Rebuilt with OVR navy #012342 in place of burgundy, gold #FEA802 as accent, stone #f1ece4 kept.
- **Spacing:** very generous, 100-130px between sections on desktop.
- **Motion:** slow crossfading hero, slider snap, soft hover. Rebuild keeps scroll-snap only and respects reduced motion.
- **Images:** large, warm, edge-to-edge; portrait crops beside text.

## Notes
- Two popups (voucher and AI chat) sit in iframes; they could not be closed from the automation browser, so the desktop capture is of the hero only.
- Their phone number and prices were not reused; the rebuild has neither.
