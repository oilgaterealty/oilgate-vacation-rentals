## What makes it recognizable
- **Layout:** dark-green utility bar ("Club Lambton Venue" / "Become A Member"), white nav with logo and a solid CTA, then a **rounded inset** aerial hero (not full bleed) with huge condensed caps ("BEYOND A CLUB, IT'S A COMMUNITY"). Two CTAs: filled pale lime and white outline. An oval stamp badge links to the sister club. A **scrolling marquee** strip of condensed caps in a pale band. Sections pair an eyebrow label + condensed headline with **arch-masked** photos.
- **Nav pattern:** utility bar + sticky white bar; hamburger on phones.
- **Type:** condensed display caps (close free match: Bebas Neue), friendly sans body (match: Montserrat).
- **Type scale:** hero ~110px desktop / ~48px phone, section heads ~70px, body 16px.
- **Palette:** dark green, sage, pale lime, white. Rebuilt with navy for the green, gold for the lime, cream #fff6e5 for the sage band.
- **Spacing:** tighter than design 1; rounded 14px corners everywhere.
- **Motion:** looping marquee, hero slider. Rebuild keeps the marquee (paused for reduced motion).
- **Images:** aerial and lifestyle photos, arch and rounded masks.
